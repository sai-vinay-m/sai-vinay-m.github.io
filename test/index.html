Manuscript in Preparation Independent Research (Extended from M.A. Dissertation)
2021 – 2026 · Telangana, India

Navigating Pest Resistance and Climate Stress: A Longitudinal Study of Bt Cotton Farmers in Telangana

  • Designed and executed multi-year longitudinal fieldwork across Adilabad and Khammam districts, applying qualitative and formative research methodologies to evaluate pink bollworm resistance, climate variability, and escalating agricultural input costs.
  • Conducted multi-stakeholder primary interviews with Bt cotton cultivators, agricultural scientists, and local extension officials to trace how ecological disruptions shape rural livelihoods and community-level adaptation.
  • Analyzing socio-ecological interactions between environmental variability, agronomic practices, and institutional response gaps in climate-stressed farming communities for peer-reviewed publication.

2026 · India

Extreme Heat and Cardiovascular Health in India: Examining Socioeconomic Inequalities in Climate Vulnerability

  • Synthesizing epidemiological and public health literature on the cardiovascular morbidity and mortality implications of extreme heat exposure across Indian populations.
  • Evaluating structural determinants of inequality across occupational heat exposure differentials, healthcare accessibility, and treatment-seeking behavior.
  • Mapping critical evidence gaps concerning climate-related cardiovascular risks among socioeconomically disadvantaged and outdoor working cohorts to inform doctoral research design.
